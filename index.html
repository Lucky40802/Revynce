About Revynce

What is Revynce?

Revynce is an AI-powered study platform for students and teachers. Students upload PDFs, documents, and images to generate AI notes, palm cards, practice tests, and quizzes. Teachers manage classes, mark rolls, post notices, build AI test papers, and track student performance - all in one place.

Google Integrations

How Revynce uses your Google data

We only request access to data strictly necessary for each feature. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties. All Google integrations are optional.

Google Sign-In

What we access: Your name, email address, and profile photo.

Why: To create and authenticate your Revynce account securely without requiring a separate password.

Google Classroom

What we access: Your enrolled courses and class rosters.

Why: To import your classes into Revynce so you can access teacher materials and classmates without re-entering your school details manually.

Google Drive

What we access: Only the specific files you choose to select.

Why: To feed your selected document into Revynce's AI engine to generate study notes, palm cards, and practice tests from your own materials.

Google Calendar

What we access: Ability to create events in your calendar.

Why: To sync your personalised study schedule and exam dates to Google Calendar so you never miss a study session.

You can use Revynce fully without connecting any Google service.  ·  Read our full Privacy Policy

About the Platform

One platform.
Every tool your school needs.

Revynce is an AI-powered education platform built for the modern classroom. Students and teachers today are forced to switch between Quizlet for flashcards, Education Perfect for practice questions, Sentral for attendance and timetables, and Google Classroom for assignments — creating friction, lost time, and fragmented data.

Revynce brings every tool into one seamless experience. Upload any PDF, worksheet, or textbook and instantly receive AI-generated notes, flashcards, quizzes, and palm cards. Manage your classes, mark the roll, post notices, check your timetable, join live voice study rooms, and compete on subject leaderboards — all without leaving the app.

      Last updated: 1 June 2026

      Revynce is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

      Data We Collect

      Account data: Name, email address, role (student/teacher), and profile preferences stored in Firebase Realtime Database.
      Study data: Uploaded file metadata, AI-generated content, quiz scores, XP, streaks, and flashcard decks.
      Class data: Enrolled classes, attendance records, test scores, and leaderboard preferences.

      Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Revynce Service. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
