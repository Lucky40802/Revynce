8 Leaderboard & Student Data Visibility

The class leaderboard ranks students by their average test and quiz scores within a class. Participation in the leaderboard is opt-in by default — students choose whether their ranking is visible to classmates when joining a class, and can change this at any time.

Teachers may enable a force-public setting for their class, which overrides individual student preferences and makes all students' rankings visible to enrolled classmates. This is disclosed to students via a clearly visible notice in the app when the setting is active.

Leaderboard data (average scores and ranking position) is only visible to students enrolled in the same class — it is never publicly accessible outside the class.
