Frequently Asked Questions
Is Revynce really free to start?
Yes — the Free plan is free forever with no time limit. You get 5 AI uploads per month, notes generation, 12 palm cards per upload, practice tests, and AI tutor chat. No credit card required.
What happens when I hit the 5 upload limit on Free?
Your limit resets at the start of each calendar month. You can still access all your previously generated notes, flashcards, and quizzes in your Archive — only new uploads are paused.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes — cancel anytime with no penalties. Your Pro or Teacher features will remain active until the end of your billing period, then your account reverts to Free. All your data is kept.
Do you offer discounts for schools?
Yes — use the Enterprise enquiry form above to get a custom quote for your school. We offer volume pricing for institutions with multiple teachers and student cohorts.
How does the 14-day free trial work?
When you sign up for a Pro or Teacher plan, your first 14 days are completely free — no charge, no card required upfront. You're only billed if you choose to continue after the trial period.
What file types can I upload?
Revynce supports PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, and image files (PNG, JPG). You can also import directly from Google Drive or OneDrive. Maximum file size is 20 MB per upload.
