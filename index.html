About the Platform

One platform.
Every tool your school needs.

Revynce is an AI-powered education platform built for the modern classroom. Students and teachers today are forced to switch between Quizlet for flashcards, Education Perfect for practice questions, Sentral for attendance and timetables, and Google Classroom for assignments — creating friction, lost time, and fragmented data.

Revynce brings every tool into one seamless experience. Upload any PDF, worksheet, or textbook and instantly receive AI-generated notes, flashcards, quizzes, and palm cards. Manage your classes, mark the roll, post notices, check your timetable, join live voice study rooms, and compete on subject leaderboards — all without leaving the app.

Revynce was born out of frustration. As a Year 10 accelerated student juggling Quizlet, Education Perfect, Sentral, and Google Classroom every single day, I kept asking — why doesn't one platform do all of this? So I built it.

I designed and developed Revynce entirely from scratch — the UI, the Firebase backend, the AI integration via Groq, the voice rooms, the Sentral-like school management features, everything. It started as my HSC Enterprise Computing accelerated major project and grew into something I genuinely believe could change how schools work.
